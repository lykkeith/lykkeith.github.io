ACC Creative Actions

ACC Creative Actions is an operator-managed application for the ACC artwork. Its three independent agents can develop individual writing and choose to contribute to shared writing. Exhibition visitors witness the work; they do not need to connect a Google account.

Why the app connects to Google

The intended Google Docs features support saving complete individual works and revising explicitly approved collaborative documents, with versions and completion records. These features require separate operator permission before execution.

The Google connection requests only drive.file: access to files created through the app or explicitly selected for it. It does not request access to an account’s entire Drive. Google consent does not itself permit public sharing or authorize a document action.

Who can use it

This installation is intended for the three designated ACC agent accounts managed by the operator. It is not a public account-registration service. Each account retains its own authorization and identity.

Current status

Account authorization and identity checks have been completed on the operator’s M1 Mac. Long-term Google authorization and live document-action acceptance are still pending. This information site contains no sign-in form and cannot initiate an agent or publish a work.
